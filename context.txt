1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Government procurement tenders across India (published via GeM, CPPP, and PSU 
portals) frequently contain ambiguous technical specifications, outdated Indian 
Standards (IS) citations, or omit critical compliance parameters. Manual standards 
verification is slow, prone to compliance errors, and vendor disputes.

StandardIQ is an on-premise, privacy-first AI intelligence engine that:
1. Ingests procurement / tender specifications in plain language.
2. Extracts domain-specific technical requirements and flags ambiguous/missing clauses.
3. Performs semantic retrieval over a curated Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) corpus.
4. Validates retrieved standards using a deterministic rule engine (checking active 
   vs. superseded editions, QCO orders, and parameter thresholds).
5. Outputs ranked, explainable recommendations backed by source clause evidence.
6. Operates 100% locally with zero data egress — safe for sensitive defense, 
   infrastructure, and government deployments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE 3-TIER INNOVATION STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Tender Spec / Query]
         │
         ▼
[Tier 1: Local Fine-Tuned LLM (LoRA/QLoRA via Ollama / llama.cpp)]
   - Understands procurement intent and technical jargon.
   - Extracts structured parameters (Voltage, Material, Environment, Application).
   - Flags missing or underspecified clauses (e.g. Missing Operating Temperature).
         │
         ▼
[Tier 2: Hybrid RAG Retrieval (BGE-M3 + ChromaDB / Qdrant)]
   - Holds live BIS standards knowledge base.
   - Encodes specifications into dense vector representations.
   - Retrieves top candidate standards without model retraining.
         │
         ▼
[Tier 3: Rule-Based Guardrail & Verification Engine]
   - Validates active status vs. superseded/withdrawn editions.
   - Checks compliance with mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCO).
   - Verifies technical rating mismatches (e.g., 750V vs 1.1kV).
         │
         ▼
[Confidence Scoring & Routing]
   ├── High (>= 80%): Recommended with source evidence and compliance badges.
   ├── Moderate (50-80%): Flagged with specific mismatch warnings for buyer review.
   └── Low (< 50%) / Superseded: Flagged as invalid/superseded; routed to human review.

   - Strict schema adherence:
     * standard_id: exact verified standard designation (e.g. "IS 1554 (Part 1): 1988")
     * title: official BIS title
     * scope_text: technical paraphrase written in original words (zero copyright infringement)
     * status: "Active" (46), "Superseded" (2), or "Withdrawn" (1)
     * sector: "Electrical"
     * normative_references: array of linked standard IDs
     * reference_type: maps references to "material_standard", "test_method", "safety_standard", 
       "installation_standard", or "terminology_standard"
     * certification: "ISI Mark (Scheme I)" (31 standards under mandatory QCO), "Voluntary", or "None"

6. BACKEND & RETRIEVAL ENGINE STATUS (backend/) [REAL RETRIEVAL & RULE PIPELINE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Framework: FastAPI + Uvicorn + Pydantic v2 + Sentence-Transformers + ChromaDB.
- Embedding Model: BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 (BGE family, 384-dim, fast local CPU inference).
  * Note: Configurable to BAAI/bge-m3 via EMBEDDING_MODEL_NAME environment variable.
- Vector Store: ChromaDB persistent client at backend/chroma_db/ indexing 49 standards.
- Tier 3 Deterministic Rule Engine [REAL]:
  * Module: backend/app/rules.py
  * Status Filter: Prioritizes "Active" standards over "Withdrawn" or "Superseded" standards.
    Inactive candidates are demoted to the bottom of the returned candidate list, and flagged
    with `superseded_warning: True` and explicit citation warnings.
  * Confidence Threshold: Deterministically configured to 0.72 (72.0%).
    Rationale: Analysis across all 18 benchmark queries showed that unambiguous, complete specs
    exhibit cosine similarity >= 0.74 (average ~0.79). Incomplete specifications (query-15: 0.7092)
    and negative-exclusion queries (query-17: 0.6799) score below 0.72, signaling ambiguity.
    Threshold 0.72 cleanly flags these 3 edge cases (16.7%) for human engineering review
    without causing false positives on unambiguous queries.
- Allied & Normative Standards In-Memory Graph [REAL - WIRED]:
  * Module: backend/app/allied_standards.py
  * Graph Engine: NetworkX DiGraph loaded at startup from standardsDataset.json.
  * Graph Topology: 52 total nodes (49 corpus standards + 3 external test method parts), 
    105 directed edges.
  * Standards with Normative References: 47 / 49 standards (95.9% of corpus).
  * Two standards without references: IS 1885 (Part 32): 1993 (vocabulary) and 
    IS 10810 (Part 0): 1984 (testing framework) — verified clean fallback (no empty UI container rendered).
  * 1-Hop Neighbor Groupings:
    - Material Specifications (material_standard)
    - Test Methods (test_method)
    - Safety & Protection (safety_standard)
    - Installation Guidelines (installation_standard)
    - Terminology & Vocabulary (terminology_standard)
  * Recommendation Attachment: Allied standards are attached ONLY to Rank #1 result; 
    Rank #2 and Rank #3 results keep `allied_standards = null` to maintain visual focus.
  * Frontend Display: Card #1 renders dedicated "Allied & Normative Standards (Cross-Referenced)"
    container with category counts, monospace standard IDs, title tags, and status dots.
- Active Endpoints:
  * GET  /health: Health check, model info, collection count (49), allied graph nodes (52), edges (105).
  * POST /recommend: Accepts {"query": "...", "top_k": 3}, returns top matches with cosine similarity,
    rule-adjusted ranks, superseded warnings, `requires_human_review` flags, and 1-hop `allied_standards` on Rank #1.
